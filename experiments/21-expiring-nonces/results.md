## 1. Spends an hour against demand

One shared sender, its spends sent by a relayer: under `one` and `expiring, one` it waits for each spend's inclusion before sending the next; under `disjoint-keys` and `expiring, many` it sends each at once. Demand and landed spends are per hour; $\rho$ is demand over the one-a-slot ceiling. Delays are seconds from the user's request to the block that included the spend. Versions are the versions of a landed spend its user signed, 1 when it was never signed again. Queued past the lead is the percentage of landed spends the relayer first sent more than 48 s after they were asked for: in the expiring variants the relayer makes a spend's version when it sends it, so each of these is a spend whose user signed when the relayer sent it, rather than when they asked. Refusals are every node's answers to the spend's versions, received from the relayer or a peer, per spend landed: busy is `ErrFrameSenderBusy` and `ErrReplaceUnderpriced`, too far `ErrFrameExpiryTooFar`.

## 2. Who submits

Section 1's relayer against `direct` users, who submit their own spend to a random node and, after every block that node imports without including it, submit it again to a random node; in the expiring variants a user whose version can no longer make the next block signs a new one first. Exposure is seconds from the spend's first submission to any node until its inclusion. Submissions, and the refusals among them, are per spend asked, from the node each submission went to. Replaced is the number of times, per spend asked, that a node dropped one of its versions for another transaction that replaced it. Upper and lower tips split the spends asked at the median tip.

## 3. The deadline

`expiring, many` with a relayer, its user setting the deadline the lead below after the timestamp of the relayer's node's head. A node refuses a version as too far when its own head is older than the relayer's by more than 60 s less the lead, and a version whose deadline is below the next block's timestamp can no longer be included.
