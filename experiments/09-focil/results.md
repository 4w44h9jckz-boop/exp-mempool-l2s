# 09. Inclusion lists and builders that filter: results

Generated by `experiments/09-focil/run.py`, seeds 1, 2, 3, 100 nodes, 8 peers each, 12 s slots, gas limit 30000000, 900 s a run, 10 builders. Do not edit by hand.

## 1. The share of builders that filter

A network of 100 nodes, 8 peers each, a block every 12 s, gas limit 30000000. Blocks are built by 10 of the nodes, drawn at random, in turn; the share in the table of those, drawn at random, filter. Three loads: background 1000000-gas transfers at 15 a slot for the whole 900 s run, the gas target on average; honest 21000-gas transfers at 6 a slot; and filtered 21000-gas transfers at 2 a slot, from senders that every filtering builder leaves out. The last two are sent for the first 450 s; every wallet draws its tip from 0.001 to 3 gwei, caps its fee at twice the next base fee plus the tip, sends to a random node, and sends once. Under `eip-7805` a committee of 16 nodes, drawn each slot, lists by fee 8 s into the slot, and a filtering builder whose block would leave a listed transaction out follows its strategy: *stuff* buys the gas that excuses it, *miss* loses the slot, *yield* lets the next builder in turn that does not filter build it, *cheapest* stuffs when the stuffed block's tips cover the burn and yields otherwise. Rows pool 3 runs, seeds 1, 2, 3.

Delays are from sending to inclusion, in seconds, over the transactions included; listed is the share of filtered transactions any includer listed. Honest p50 is the honest transfers' delay, background included the share of background transfers sent in the first 450 s that were included by the end of the run. Slots are those a filtering builder was due, with how many it stuffed, yielded and missed, and what filtering cost it: the priority fees of the block it would have built honestly from the same pool and lists, less those it was paid, plus the base fee it burned, in ETH and as a share of those honest fees. Base fees are in gwei: the highest in any run, and the median of the runs' last.

## 4. Private spends

Section 1's network and loads, with the filtered transfers replaced by spends: self-paying frame transactions whose structural rule 6 budget is 211828, topic 25889's single-nullifier spend as in experiment 08, the proof in a 256-byte ARBITRARY entry, each from a sender of its own, at 2 a slot for the first 450 s, sent once. *Filtered*: every node admits up to 250000, every spend goes to a random node, and 0.9 of the builders leave spends out and yield. *Floor*: experiment 08's regime, with every node at the floor, 100000, except 10% of the nodes, drawn at random from those that do not build, which admit up to 250000; every spend goes to one of those, and no builder filters. Under `eip-8369` includers list a spend as a Profile 2 candidate within their list's budget (IL-2 to IL-4); `eip-7805` lists no frame transaction.
