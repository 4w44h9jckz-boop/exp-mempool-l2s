## 1. The EIP's window

50 nodes, 8 peers each, one-way link latency uniform in 20 to 100 ms, gas limit 30000000, base fee from 1 gwei. Each slot (12 s) one of 10 builders is drawn at random to build the block, and those named filtering leave out what the row says. Frame pool profile `eip-8272`, with `recent_root_length` and `root_margin` as given. A root source writes a fresh root every slot from 3 s. From 60 s to 1500 s users ask for one spend a slot on average, each from a sender of its own; a user reads the latest root written at or before the head of a random node it then watches, proves for 12 s, and sends the spend to a random node, again after every block its node imports without including it, and proves again against the latest root when a node refuses the spend for its root. The run lasts 1800 s. Rows pool 5 runs, seeds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, each with its own topology, builders and draw of proposers. Here `recent_root_length` is the EIP's 8192, so the usable window is 8191 slots. The congested row adds 1,000,000-gas transfers at the gas target for the whole run and 3 a second more from 600 s to 900 s, when demand is 1.7 times the gas limit; with `eip-8369` a filtering builder whose block would leave a listed spend out yields the slot to the next builder that does not filter (decision record 0018). In the last row every builder leaves the root writes out from 600 s.

Delay is from asking to inclusion, in seconds, proving included. Ages are in slots: a root's when its spend is first sent, at the node the user watches, and when the spend lands. Slots to spare is 8191 less the oldest root that was sent or landed. Proofs are per spend, and evictions are the pools that dropped one of its versions for its root's age (RR-7), per spend.
