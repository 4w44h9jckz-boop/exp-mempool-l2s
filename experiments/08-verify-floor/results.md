## 1. The share of nodes with headroom

A network of 100 nodes, 8 peers each, a block every 12 s from a proposer that rotates through every node, gas limit 30000000. Every node runs `eip-8141` with `max_verify_gas` at the floor, 100000, except the share in the table, drawn at random, which admits up to 250000. Wallets send spends whose structural rule 6 budget is 211828, the single-nullifier spend of topic 25889, its proof in a 256-byte ARBITRARY entry, each from a sender of its own, at 2 a slot for the first 1200 s of a 2400 s run, to a random node with headroom, which is where a wallet that tries random nodes until one accepts ends up; they send each once. Rows pool 3 runs, seeds 1, 2, 3, each with its own topology and placement. ## 4. Budget hints

Section 1's network with 50% of nodes at random with headroom. Each run carries four kinds of spend: budgets of 211828 and of 100000, each with a 256-byte proof, which a node pushes to some peers in full, and with a 6000-byte one, over go-ethereum's 4096-byte `txMaxBroadcastSize`, which it only announces. Spends above the floor go to a random node with headroom, spends at the floor to a random honest node. With hints, every announcement carries the spend's rule 6 budget, and a node does not fetch a spend announced above its own `max_verify_gas`; it does not refuse what it is pushed.

Nodes that do not want spends to travel, drawn at random from those at the floor, in the share the table names: they admit nothing, so they relay nothing, and the blocks they propose carry no spend. Without hints that is all they can do (*keep quiet*). With hints they announce every spend they hear of to every peer with a budget above any node's (*lie*), and a node that hears the lie either forgets it once it has not fetched (*lie, forgotten*) or, as it does an underpriced transaction, ignores every announcement of the spend for 300 s (*lie, remembered*).
