## 1. Payments against withholding publishers

Experiment 07's network: 10 nodes, 8 peers each, a block every 12 s from a rotating proposer, gas limit 60000000, every pool at the profile's 4096 slots under the policy in the table. Payments arrive at the rate in the table, each from a sender of its own with one in flight, bidding no ToB fee, tolerating 31 commitments before them, 100000 gas, sent to random nodes from the start until second 960, and sent again to a random node after every block that does not commit them; trades arrive at 2 a slot as in experiment 07, and transfers of 1000000 gas fill the rest of the gas target. Each payment's attempt names one of 10 key publishers at random, among those that have not withheld from its sender before; the share in the table withhold every key. When the block that committed a withheld ticket reaches the node the sender sent it to, the sender sends the payment again at its next nonce: sealed, re-encrypted to a new publisher, or public, as a regular transaction with the same gas and tip. Rows are the payments first sent from second 240 to second 960; the run lasts 1200 s. Executed is by the end of the run, sealed or public; delays are from first sending to the block that runs the payment, its decrypted transaction one block after its commitment or its regular transaction, in seconds. Attempts is the mean number of the payment's tickets committed; lost is the mean, and the largest, wei a payment paid for tickets whose key was withheld; commitments and withheld are the mean a block made, and of them the withheld, during the window.
