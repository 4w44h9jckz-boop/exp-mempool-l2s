## 0. Committee sizes

A committee of $n$ drawn from validators of which 1/3 are corrupt has no honest majority when at least half its members are corrupt; the chance, from the binomial distribution, exactly:

| n | chance of no honest majority | log2 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 16 | 1.265e-01 | -2.98 |
| 64 | 4.266e-03 | -7.87 |
| 423 | 8.165e-13 | -40.16 |

The smallest $n$ whose chance is below $2^{-40}$ is 423. 16 is EIP-7805's `IL_COMMITTEE_SIZE`.
