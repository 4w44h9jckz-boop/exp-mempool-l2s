## 2. Upgrading 16 busy accounts

16 accounts without code, each sending plain transfers at the rate below, at once, from a wallet that sends each account's transfers to one node, and batches them, as section 1's batch, once its node's head shows the account delegated. From second 1800 a sponsor upgrades them in type 4 transactions of the batch size below, at a tip of 2 gwei, sent to one node. Each authorization is signed at its account's next nonce by a wallet that keeps sending; that pauses, sending nothing more for the account until its node shows the delegation or the sponsor reports the authorization skipped; or that drains, pausing as well but signing only once nothing it sent for the account is waiting for a block. The sponsor sends a batch again after its node's next import when the node refused it or a wallet had not signed, and moves on once a block includes it, keeping in line any account whose authorization the block skipped (BB-5). Delegated is accounts delegated by the end, 2 hours, and time the seconds from second 1800 to the last. Sends are type 4 transactions sent; reserved the sponsor's node's `ErrAuthorityReserved`, and later those after its first send; peers the other nodes' `ErrAuthorityReserved` to them, per send; skipped the authorizations a block skipped, and their gas, 25,000 each, which the sponsor paid; lost the transfers sent again because an authorization used their nonce; NP-6 the wallet's node's refusals. Delays are seconds from the request to inclusion, before for transfers asked from second 600 to the start, and during for those asked in the 600 s after it. Unlanded is transfers asked more than a minute before the end and not included.
