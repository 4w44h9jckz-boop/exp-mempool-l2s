## 1. A self-issuer's junk proofs

30 nodes, 8 peers each, a block every 12 s from a rotating proposer, under EIP-8288 with claims (CG-1), experiment 24's `laptop, budget` nodes. Wallets send 8 spends a slot, each with one leanSTARK dependency, as mode 0 wrappers, for 1236 s of a 1536 s run, epochs of 384 s. Each spend carries a valid ticket of one of 4 honest issuers and pays for itself, which sets aside FP-11's cap of one pending transaction for a ticket paymaster. From second 60 until second 1152 a self-issuer hands every node 4 wrappers a slot, each from a fresh sender with a valid ticket it signed, whose proof fails. With more than one paymaster it moves to the next at a node that refuses the current one. Each prefix runs the paymaster's `VERIFY` frame, which spent 122006 gas on the testnet, and a ticket check is the same pairing outside the EVM. `none` is version 4 as written; `memory k=n` closes an issuer at a node, for the rest of the epoch, at its n-th failed proof there; `list` accepts only the honest issuers. Ticket checks, prefixes and proofs count what nodes ran on its wrappers; a node an epoch is the mean of the proofs one node verified in each of epochs 1 and 2, which the attack covers whole. Deployment gas is 2443085 a paymaster it used; a proof is that over the proofs nodes verified in the run, and over a day that over 225 epochs at a node an epoch's rate on every node, with the same paymasters. Float is the least each must hold, 383893005374502 wei; it has no withdrawal and leaves only as gas the paymaster approves. Proof s prices the proofs at 11.442 ms each. Honest refused counts the times a node refused an honest spend's issuer.

## 2. A holder of honest tickets

Section 1's network and wallets, and no self-issuer. A holder of h0's tickets, bought at 1000000000000000 wei each, hands every node, 5 s into each epoch from second 60 until second 1152, wrappers of its own whose proofs fail, one for each ticket, n tickets an epoch. Nodes closed is the mean, over epochs 1 and 2, of the nodes that closed h0; seconds closed the mean time from a node's closing to the epoch's end. Honest refused counts the times a node refused an honest spend because its issuer was closed, and honest tickets the tickets wallets bought beyond those of `none`, a fresh one whenever the epoch moved on before their spend landed. The last two columns are the delays of the other issuers' spends.
