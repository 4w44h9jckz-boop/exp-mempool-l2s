# 12. Propagation as a timing channel: results

Generated by `experiments/12-timing-channel/run.py`, seeds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 50 nodes and in section 3 25, 50, 100, 200, 8 peers each and in section 2 4, 8, 16, 12 s slots, 720 s a run, frame pool profile `hegota-testnet`. Do not edit by hand.

## 1. Who the observers find, and how soon

50 nodes, each dialling random peers until it has 8, one-way link latency uniform in 20 to 100 ms, arrive timeout 0.5 s, frame pool profile `hegota-testnet`, a block every 12 s from a proposer that rotates through the nodes, gas limit 30000000. From 12 s to 420 s users send spends from one shared sender, 3 a slot, each to a random node, with a 1 gwei tip; the run lasts 720 s. A spy is connected to every node and relays nothing; observers are a share of the nodes, drawn at random, which relay as every node does. Rows pool 5 runs, seeds 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, each with its own topology and observers.

For each spend the observers take the first time any of them heard of it, by a push, an announcement or a fetch's answer, and guess that the peer they heard it from is the node the user handed it to. At an observer counts spends handed to an observer, which it knows for certain and which the other columns leave out. Origin found is the share of the rest the guess names; origin or a peer of it, the share it names the node or one of its peers; chance, one over the nodes the origin could be. Heard after is from the user's hand-over to the observers' first hearing, in seconds, and held before inclusion from then until the block that includes the spend.
