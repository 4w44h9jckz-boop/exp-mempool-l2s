## 1. Copies of one spend

50 nodes, 8 peers each, a block every 12 s from a proposer that rotates through every node, gas limit 30000000, frame pool profile `hegota-testnet`. From 12 s to 420 s users ask for spends from one shared sender at one a slot, each with a 1 gwei tip and a fee cap of twice the next base fee plus the tip, and hand each out at once as copies, each to a different random node, or one to every node. The run lasts 480 s. Rows pool 3 runs, seeds 1, 2, 3, each with its own topology.

Nullifier says where the spend's nullifier is: its nonce key, or a slot of the sender's storage. Static first is FP-1's `static_first`. Per spend: copies handed out; pools that held a copy; pools that held a copy after another copy's block was built, which has lost; prefix runs at admission, admitting or refusing, across all nodes and copies; refusals; re-runs of a lost copy at a new head (FP-15); and attempts by builders that failed (BB-3). Held after loss is, for each pool and lost copy, from the winning block's building until the pool dropped the copy, in seconds. Other re-runs are those of spends not yet included, per node and block. Delay is from asking to the winning block.
