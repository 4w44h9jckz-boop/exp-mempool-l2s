## 1. Delay at low demand

A network of 30 nodes, 8 peers each, a block every 12 s from a rotating proposer, gas limit 6300000. Wallets, each its own default-code sender, send 2 transactions a slot to random nodes for 25 minutes; a wallet with one in flight waits, and after every block that leaves it out sends it again to a random node. Each transaction is `[dependency, self_verify, sender]` with one leanSTARK triple, sent as a mode 0 wrapper with its proof: max_gas 103849, 133849 of a block with AG-8's charge. The gossip row sends the same transactions without the dependency frame (max_gas 71838) under `eip-8141`, as ordinary transactions. Every node aggregates once every 1 s at a phase of its own and sends after the proving delay, if any, plus the link's latency. Reached is the number of transactions that became pending at half the nodes and at all of them, with the median time from first sending; delays are from first sending to inclusion, in seconds; and the last four columns say how the block's proposer had first admitted each included transaction: by ordinary gossip, from a user's wrapper sent to it, from a peer's aggregate, or from a forwarded user's wrapper. Carried is the mean, over the slots of the demand, of the distinct transactions listed in any node's aggregate during the slot.

## 4. What nodes send each other
