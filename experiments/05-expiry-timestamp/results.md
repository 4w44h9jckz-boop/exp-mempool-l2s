# 05. The expiry timestamp: results

Generated by `experiments/05-expiry-timestamp/run.py`, seed 1, 12 s slots, gas limit 2100000, 10 nodes in sections 2 and 3. Do not edit by hand.

## 1. One pool and the next block

A frame pool of capacity 4096 at the pending base fee of 1 gwei and the head's timestamp is filled with honest transactions from distinct senders, a third each of three classes, as in experiment 04: *pays*, a tip of 0.001 to 3 gwei and a fee cap of twice the base fee plus the tip; *deadline*, the same with a deadline 600 s out; *stranded*, a fee cap below the base fee. Then as many again of the adversary's transactions arrive, each from a sender of its own: *free*, a fee cap and tip of 1 wei, paid by a canonical paymaster instance; *expiring*, the earliest deadline the pool admits, the head's timestamp plus `expiry_margin`, at a tip of 10 gwei and a fee cap of twice the base fee plus the tip, paid by the same paymaster; *drained*, priced as *expiring* without a deadline, paid by one default-code sponsor, which then sends its whole balance away in a 21,000-gas transfer at 20 gwei. Then the pool builds the next block, 12 s later. Next block counts the adversary's transactions and the honest ones it includes, skipped is the adversary's that the builder skipped and why, and paid is what the adversary paid for its included ones, in wei.

## 2. A flood on the network

Wallets that pay for themselves send 1500 frame transactions an hour to random nodes, half of them with a deadline 600 s after first sending; each has one in flight, sends it again to a random node after every block that leaves it out, and gives it up once its deadline has passed, as in experiment 04. Every node's frame pool holds at most 256. From minute 10 to minute 20 an adversary keeps every pool full of its transactions, sent to each node directly at the start and after every block it imports: enough to fill it and 16 more, of the kinds of section 1, *expiring* or *drained*, made afresh at every head. The run lasts 45 minutes. Rows are the honest transactions first sent during the flood: landed in the flood is before minute 20, delays are of the landed ones in seconds from first sending, overflow is `ErrTxPoolOverflow` answers per transaction, and evicted is FP-14 evictions per transaction across nodes.
