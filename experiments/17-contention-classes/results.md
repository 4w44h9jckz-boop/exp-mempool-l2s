## 2. Mass invalidation

Experiment 02's malicious application: it earns width with 600 paid spends an hour for the first 45 minutes, and from minute 60 to minute 80, after every block its node n05 imports, sends up to 100 junk spends at a tip of 1 wei, which no builder includes, while an account it controls sends a transaction that changes, in the next block, what the junk read. With one setting every junk spend reads slot 1 of the application and the flip is experiment 02's, 26,000 gas. With a setting each, junk spend $i$ reads slot $1 + (i \bmod 100)$; with an application each, junk spend $i$ comes from application $i \bmod 100$ and reads slot 1 of its own storage. In both the flip writes every setting a junk spend held at n05 reads, for 21,000 gas, 5,000 a slot and 2,600 for each further application (`sim/emem_sim/workload.py`). Per block of the attack: junk admitted at n05, and per node the re-simulations and the work for the adversary's senders, in gas; the attacker's gas is what its flips used, per block; the ratio is work per node against it; width is n05's for the first sender.
