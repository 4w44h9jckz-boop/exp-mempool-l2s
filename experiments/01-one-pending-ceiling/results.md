## 1. Spends an hour against demand

One shared sender, its spends sent by a relayer: under `one` it waits for each spend's inclusion before sending the next, under `disjoint-keys` it sends each at once. Demand and landed spends are per hour; $\rho$ is demand over the one-a-slot ceiling. Delays are seconds from the user's request to the block that included the spend; the formula is the mean of a slotted queue that serves one spend a slot (`one`, $\frac{s/2}{1-\rho}$) or all of them (`disjoint-keys`, $s/2$). A slot is missed when no spend was included though one had been waiting since before the previous slot. Busy answers are every node's `ErrFrameSenderBusy` to a spend, received from the relayer or a peer, per spend landed. Re-simulations are FP-15's, per node per block.

## 2. Who submits

One shared sender under `one`. `relayer` is section 1's; `direct` users submit their own spend to a random node and, after every block that node imports without including it, submit it again to a random node. The `disjoint-keys` relayer is the reference. Exposure is seconds from the spend's first submission to any node until its inclusion, the window in which its nullifier is public. Submissions, and the `ErrFrameSenderBusy` answers to them, are per spend asked, from the node each submission went to. Spends held is the number of distinct spends pending at any node just before a slot, and the last column the share of nodes holding none then.
