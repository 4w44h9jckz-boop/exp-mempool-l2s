# 13. MATCHA's load floor: results

Generated by `experiments/13-load-floor/run.py`, seeds 1, 2, 3, 50 nodes, 8 peers each, 12 s slots, 1800 s a run, frame pool profile `matcha` with MA-13's `load_price`. Do not edit by hand.

## 1. Wallets that meet the floor

50 nodes, 8 peers each, one-way link latency uniform in 20 to 100 ms, a block every 12 s from a proposer that rotates through every node, gas limit 30000000, base fee from 1 gwei. Frame pool profile `matcha` with MA-13's `load_price` as in the table, 0 being the profile's own. From 12 s to 1680 s two populations send the same spend at the same rate, each to a random node: users of one shared sender, which every node credits with the width cap before the run, and users who each have a sender of their own, drawn from 20000. Each user draws a tip uniform in 0.001 to 3 gwei and offers the higher of it and the floor the node it sends to asks of it; the fee cap is twice the next base fee plus the tip, and a spend a block leaves out is sent again to a random node at the same fees. The run lasts 1800 s. Rows pool 3 runs, seeds 1, 2, 3, each with its own topology.

Tips are in gwei a gas and are means per spend. Premium is what a user offered over what it drew; raised, the share of spends whose floor was above the tip drawn. Refusals are per spend and summed over nodes: for the floor (`ErrFrameLoadUnderpriced`) where a node's load was higher than at the node the user read, and for width (`ErrFrameInsufficientWidth`). Delay is from asking to inclusion, in seconds.
