# 20. A committee that clears every block: results

Generated by `experiments/20-per-block-clearing/run.py`, seed 1, 20 nodes, 8 peers each, gas limit 60000000, 1200 s a run, frame pool profile `eip-8141`. Do not edit by hand.

## 1. Orders from the chain

20 nodes, 8 peers each, a block every slot from a proposer in turn among the nodes that are not the committee's, gas limit 60000000, transfers of 1000000 gas filling the gas target beside the orders and one settlement a block. Users send orders of 100000 gas at 0.5 a second, from accounts of their own, at a tip of 3000000000 wei and a fee cap of 100 times the next base fee, to random nodes, until second 1080. The committee's members are nodes n17, n18, n19. Each batch takes the orders a block carried, starts once every member has imported that block and the last batch has ended, takes the rounds in the table at a 80 ms round trip, and is settled by n17 with a settlement of 1125950 gas from an account, at a tip of 2000000000 wei, meant for the next block. Rows are the batches meant for a block from second 120 to second 1080 and the orders sent in it; the run lasts 1200 s. In time is the percentage of batches settled in the block they were meant for, and late the most blocks after it; next block is the percentage of orders included in the first block after they were sent; delays are seconds from sending an order to the block that settled it.
