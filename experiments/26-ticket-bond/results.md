## 1. A self-issuer's junk proofs against a bond

Experiment 25's network: 30 nodes, 8 peers each, a block every 12 s from a rotating proposer, under EIP-8288 with claims (CG-1). Wallets send 8 spends a slot, each with one leanSTARK dependency, as mode 0 wrappers, with valid tickets of 4 busy honest issuers, and 4 spends an epoch with tickets of a small one, s0, for 6996 s of a 7296 s run. From second 60 until second 6912 a self-issuer hands every node 4 wrappers a slot, each from a fresh sender with a valid ticket it signed, whose proof fails, moving to its next paymaster at a node that refuses the current one. `none` is version 4 as written. Under `bond` every node runs the `erc-8421` descriptor's reputation for ticket paymasters: it refuses an unstaked paymaster's tickets, and a paymaster's state is `BANNED` once `seen // 10` exceeds `included + 50`, with both counters multiplied by 23/24 every 3600 s. In `bond, unbonded` the self-issuer's paymaster has no stake; in `bond` each has 1000000000000000000 wei staked with an unstake delay of 86400 s. Hour 1 and hour 2 are the junk proofs one node verified in each, the mean over nodes; banned at is the median time a node first banned the first paymaster, over the nodes that did; banned share is the share of the attack's node-time its paymasters spent banned. Stake ETH-days is what it locks, from the attack's start to the end of the unstake delay after it stops. Proof s prices the proofs at 11.442 ms each.

## 3. What a bond costs a small honest issuer

Derived from the descriptor's parameters, not simulated. The bond is 1000000000000000000 wei, which is 1000 tickets' face value at 1000000000000000 wei, locked from the first ticket to 86400 s after the last. Against griefing, an issuer whose spends a node counts r times in an hour, every one included, is throttled there by the least J junk proofs with `(r + J) // 10 > r + 10` and banned by the least with `(r + J) // 10 > r + 50`, on counters that start at zero and before a refresh. A ticket buys one junk proof at each node an epoch (CG-4 refuses its slot after it fails), so J is also the tickets an attacker needs, sent to every node within the hour, at 1000000000000000 wei each. Section 2 checks two points of it by simulation.
