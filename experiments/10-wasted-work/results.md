Generated by `experiments/10-wasted-work/run.py`, seeds 1, 2, 3, 25, 50, 100, 200 nodes, 8 peers each, 12 s slots, 360 s a run, frame pool profiles `eip-8141` and `hegota-testnet`. Do not edit by hand.

## 1. Sponsors that hold nothing

Networks of the size in the table, 8 peers each, a block every 12 s from a proposer that rotates through every node, gas limit 30000000. Honest wallets send self-paying frame transactions at 6 a slot for the whole 360 s run, as the yardstick. From 36 s to 324 s the adversary sends 12 sponsored transactions a slot, each from a default-code sender of its own and backed by a default-code sponsor of its own that holds nothing, at a fee cap of twice the next base fee plus a 1 gwei tip, and hands each to one random node or to every node. Rows pool 3 runs, seeds 1, 2, 3, each with its own topology.

Handed counts what the adversary handed a node, a transaction handed to every node once for each. Runs are the prefixes nodes ran to admit or refuse a transaction (FP-8, before FP-11), re-runs those they ran again at a new head (FP-15), and work the two together: per hand-over, per transaction and node, and for an honest transaction per node. Pools reached is how many nodes held a transaction pending; landed how many a block included.

## 2. A sponsor drained every block

Section 1's networks and honest wallets. The same money backs every round. Failed in block is how many times a builder tried a transaction and failed it, per transaction.
