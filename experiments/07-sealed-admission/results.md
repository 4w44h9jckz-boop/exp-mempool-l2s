## 1. One pool and the next block

A sealed pool of the profile's size, 4096 slots, at a head with gas limit 60000000 and base fee 1000000000 wei, holds a slot's honest demand: 16 payments that bid no ToB fee and tolerate 31 commitments before them, 100000 gas each, and 2 trades of 300000 gas that bid a ToB fee drawn between 1000000000000 and 1000000000000000 wei and tolerate between 1 and 8. A flood then sends enough of its sealed transactions to fill the pool and 64 more, each from a sender of its own, tolerating no commitment before it, 256 bytes and so 16384 gas: *cheap* ones that all bid 1 wei of ToB fee, or *escalating* ones that bid from 1000000000000001 wei up, one more each. Then one more honest payment arrives, and the pool's node builds the next block and resets to it. Paid is what the flood's commitments cost it once revealed, in wei, and capital is the balance its senders must hold for the pool to admit them all (LU-5).
