# 14. ERC-8421's standard alternative mempool

Version 1, 2026-10-20.

**Status: read from a Draft.** ERC-8421, "Frame Transaction Alternative
Mempools", is read from ERCs pull request 2028 at `493ba8a` (Draft, created
2026-09-21, read 2026-10-20). It defines the **standard alternative
mempool**, "a default ruleset for a permissionless and decentralized public
Frame Transactions mempool that is less restrictive than the canonical one",
with stake and reputation for the entities a transaction's validation runs.
This document reads each of its rules into the model of `00-model.md`
(version 10) as a module of `13-descriptors.md`, named as the ERC names it,
and `docs/spec/descriptors/erc-8421.json` is the descriptor that holds them.
Every clause names the rules it reads. A choice the ERC leaves open says
*Interpretation* and names decision record 0061.

The ERC has three kinds of mempool: EIP-8141's canonical one, this standard
one, and others operators define. Its standard ruleset "extends the public
mempool rather than replacing it": a pool under it is the frame pool of
`02-frame-pool.md` with six of its rule modules replaced (AM-2).

**Admission.** A pool under the ruleset checks a transaction in this order,
the first failure being the result: FP-2; FP-3, with SIGNATURE-010 (AM-6);
the structure (AM-4); the entities, their stake and reputation (AM-3,
AM-13); the budgets (AM-5); the simulation and trace (AM-7 to AM-9);
solvency (AM-12); the local rules (AM-11); the nonce, then the limits
(AM-10); the replacement (AM-10); FP-13 and FP-14. **Source:** the ERC's Acceptance
Algorithm, steps 1 to 7. Where FP-3's static checks come in it is not
said; they need no state, so they come first, as in EIP-8141.

**AM-3. Entities and stake.** A transaction's **entities** are its
**sender**, `tx.sender`; its **payer**, the resolved target of the frame
that carries `APPROVE_PAYMENT` (FP-4's last prefix frame); and its
**factory**, the target of its `deploy` frame, if it has one. Every
validation frame is attributed to one entity: an approving frame to its
resolved target's role, a `pre_verify` frame to the entity of the approving
frame after it, a `deploy` frame to the factory, and an `expiry_verify`
frame to none. An entity is **default-code** if its account's `code` is
empty at the pool's head; such an entity is never staked. An entity is
**staked** if its account's `stake` is at least `min_stake_value`, its
`unstake_delay` at least `min_unstake_delay`, and its `withdrawing` false
(MD-2, version 10): what `getDepositInfo` at `staking_registry` returns
for it. **Source:** Definitions 4 to 6; [STAKING-010]. *Interpretation*,
decision record 0061: the model keeps the registry's answer on the account
rather than in the registry's storage.

**AM-4. Structure.** Frames of the prefix are classified as in FP-5, with
two changes for a frame FP-5 calls `deploy`, a `DEFAULT` frame with no
approval scope: it is `pre_verify` if its resolved target is the resolved
target of the approving `VERIFY` frame immediately after it; otherwise it is
`deploy` if it is the prefix's first frame, not counting a leading
`expiry_verify` frame, and **stray** if not. The pool MUST reject, in this
order:

1. with `ErrFrameExpiryNotFirst`, an `expiry_verify` frame anywhere but
   first; with `ErrFrameNoPayer`, a transaction with no frame carrying
   `APPROVE_PAYMENT` [PREFIX-010];
2. with `ErrFrameAtomicInPrefix`, a prefix frame with `ATOMIC_BATCH_FLAG`
   [PREFIX-040];
3. with `ErrFrameStrayDefault`, a stray frame [PREFIX-120], which is also
   every `deploy` frame PREFIX-020 does not allow, since only the first
   frame can be one;
4. with `ErrFrameUnrecognizedPrefix`, a prefix that, without a leading
   `expiry_verify` frame and without its `pre_verify` frames, is not one of
   FP-5's four shapes [PREFIX-010]; no approving frame can have two
   `pre_verify` frames, since only the frame immediately before it is one;
5. with `ErrFrameVerifyAfterPrefix`, a `VERIFY` frame after the prefix
   [PREFIX-050];
6. with `ErrFramePreVerifyNoCode`, a `pre_verify` frame whose target has
   empty code at the pool's head [PREFIX-110], the only step that reads
   state.

*Interpretation*, decision record 0061: a `DEFAULT` frame whose resolved
target is that of the approving frame immediately after it is `pre_verify`
wherever it stands. The ERC tells the two apart by whether the frame creates
code at `tx.sender`, which a node learns only by simulating, and by the
target, which it knows before.

**AM-5. Budgets.** For each entity the pool MUST sum `exec_gas` over the
validation frames attributed to it (AM-3), adding to the sender's sum the
signature verification cost of FEE-4. A sum above
`max_verify_gas_staked_entity` for a staked entity, or above
`max_verify_gas_per_entity` for any other, is refused with
`ErrEntityVerifyGas`. No cap applies to `state_gas`. **Source:**
[BUDGET-010]; Rationale, "No cap on state gas". *Interpretation*, decision
record 0061: the `expiry_verify` frame is attributed to no entity, so its
gas counts against none.

**AM-6. Signatures.** A transaction with any signature that is not `valid`
is refused with `ErrInvalidSender` before any frame is simulated, which is
FP-3's check; under this ruleset it names [SIGNATURE-010].

**AM-7. Simulation.** The prefix is simulated as FP-8 does, with these
readings:

- a frame that reverts, or a `VERIFY` frame that does not call `APPROVE`
  with its scope, is refused with `ErrFramePrefixReverted` or
  `ErrFrameMissingApprove` [PREFIX-060]; a `pre_verify` frame that reverts
  is refused with `ErrFramePrefixReverted` too, since "mempool nodes reject
  at admission a transaction whose `pre_verify` frame does not succeed in
  simulation" (Execution Model);
- a payer that cannot cover `max_cost` makes `APPROVE` revert,
  `ErrInsufficientFunds` [PREFIX-060];
- an `expiry_verify` frame whose deadline is below the head's timestamp
  plus `expiry_margin` is refused with `ErrFrameExpired` [PREFIX-080];
- a `deploy` frame that installs no code at `sender` is refused with
  `ErrFrameDeployNoCode` [PREFIX-020];
- a frame whose target is default-code, the canonical paymaster
  (`canonical_paymaster_code`) or the expiry verifier is evaluated
  directly, as FP-8 does, and the trace rules of AM-8 and AM-9 do not
  apply to it [PREFIX-100];
- a `pre_verify` frame's writes are seen by the frames after it.

**AM-8. Trace rules.** For every other validation frame the pool MUST
reject, in this order:

- with `ErrFrameUnassignedOpcode`, an observation of `UNASSIGNED`
  [OPCODES-030];
- with `ErrFrameOutOfGasRevert`, an observation of `OOG_REVERT`
  [OPCODES-040];
- with `ErrFrameBannedOpcode`, an observation of an opcode on EIP-8141's
  banned list (FP-9) other than `CREATE`, `CREATE2`, `SETDELEGATE` and
  `SSTORE`, except `BALANCE` and `SELFBALANCE` in a frame attributed to a
  staked entity [OPCODES-010, OPCODES-020];
- with `ErrFrameCreation`, `CREATE`, `CREATE2` or `SETDELEGATE` outside the
  `deploy` frame [CREATION-010];
- with `ErrFrameCallTarget`, a call to an address that is not `sender`, not
  among `precompiles`, and whose code is empty [CALLING-010, CALLING-040].

The model writes `UNASSIGNED` for any unassigned opcode a frame executes and
`OOG_REVERT` for a revert caused by running out of gas, as a node's tracer
would observe them (MD-6, version 10). *Interpretation*, decision record
0061: CALLING-010's "an address that has no deployed or default code" is
read as one with empty code, since every address with empty code runs the
default code only as a frame's target, and a call runs none; unlike FP-9,
an EIP-7702 delegation counts as code. *Not modelled*: CREATION-010's "at
most once", since a frame's observed opcodes are a set.

**AM-9. Storage.** Each read and write of a validation frame names its
address and slot, and the address, if any, the slot is **associated** with
as the node's tracer finds it [ASSOC-010, ASSOC-020]. Writes are possible
only in `deploy` and `pre_verify` frames; a `VERIFY` frame's write makes it
revert, `ErrFramePrefixReverted` (Execution Model). An access is allowed,
and every other is refused with `ErrFrameStorageAccess` [STORAGE-000], if:

- it is to `sender`'s own storage [STORAGE-010];
- it is to storage associated with `sender` in a contract that is not an
  entity of the transaction, and the transaction has no `deploy` frame
  [STORAGE-021] or its factory is staked [STORAGE-022];
- the frame's entity is staked, and the access is to its own storage
  [STORAGE-031], a read of any storage of a contract that is not an entity
  [STORAGE-032], or a write to a slot associated with the entity in a
  contract that is not an entity [STORAGE-033].

Within a frame, AM-8's checks come first, then a `VERIFY` frame's write,
then each access, its reads in order and then its writes, and then FP-8's
outcome: a revert, a read whose `expected` value the state does not hold,
or a missing approval. A write of a `pre_verify` or `deploy` frame is in the
state the later frames read. *Interpretation*, decision record 0061: a
slot's association is an observation, like the frame's other observations;
`ASSOC-020`'s range of 0 to 128 is a parameter of the tracer that makes it,
which the descriptor carries.

**AM-10. Nonces, conflicts and limits.** A pending transaction is
identified by `(sender, nonce_keys, nonce_seq)`. For each key in its
`nonce_keys`, a transaction's `nonce_seq` MUST be the sender's current
sequence for that key or one more than the `nonce_seq` of a pending
transaction of the sender that selects that key, else it is refused with
`ErrNonceTooLow` if it is below the current sequence and `ErrNonceGap`
otherwise [LIFECYCLE-010]. A transaction **conflicts** with a pending one of
the same sender with the same `nonce_seq` whose `nonce_keys` share a key; it
replaces every one it conflicts with, and MUST raise both fees by
`price_bump` percent over each, or be refused with `ErrReplaceUnderpriced`;
on its admission the pool removes those it replaces, and every pending
transaction of the sender that no longer meets LIFECYCLE-010 [LIFECYCLE-020].
When FP-14 evicts a transaction to admit another, the pool likewise removes
every pending transaction of the evicted one's sender that then has a gap.
Not counting those it replaces, the pool MUST refuse with
`ErrNonceKeyLimit` a transaction for which some selected `(sender, key)`
already has `same_nonce_key_mempool_count` pending transactions, and with
`ErrSenderLimit` one whose unstaked sender already has
`same_sender_mempool_count` [LIFECYCLE-030]. The nonce is checked here, in
step 6 of the Acceptance Algorithm, and not before the simulation as FP-7
does.

**AM-11. Local rules.** The pool MUST refuse with `ErrEntityIsSender` a
transaction whose factory or payer, when not its sender, is the sender of a
pending transaction [LOCAL-010], and with `ErrLocalStorage` one whose
validation frames access storage associated with its sender or with a
staked entity of it, in a contract that is the sender of another pending
transaction [LOCAL-020]. Such a refusal changes no reputation.

**AM-12. Solvency.** FP-11's exposure check, with $R(P)$ the summed
`max_cost` of the payer's pending transactions not counting those this one
replaces and $w(P)$ the withdrawal of a canonical paymaster: refused with
`ErrFramePayerExposure` if $\mathit{balance}(P) - R(P) - w(P)$ is below the
transaction's `max_cost` [SOLVENCY-010, SOLVENCY-020]. A replacement that
names another payer moves the reservation with it [SOLVENCY-030]. There is
no cap of FP-11's kind on a paymaster's pending transactions; AM-13's
allowance takes its place.

**AM-13. Reputation.** For each entity address the pool keeps two counters,
`seen` and `included`, which start as the node was given them, 0 unless said
otherwise, and every `refresh_seconds` of the pool's time, counted from 0,
multiplies both by 23 and divides by 24, rounding down. The pool exposes an
entity's counters and its state (MD-9). With
$m = \lfloor \mathit{seen} / \mathit{min\_inclusion\_rate\_denominator} \rfloor$,
an entity is `BANNED` if $m > \mathit{included} + \mathit{ban\_slack}$,
`THROTTLED` if $\mathit{included} + \mathit{throttling\_slack} < m$ and it is
not banned, and `ACTIVE` otherwise. At admission the pool checks each
entity once, in the order sender, payer, factory, counting the pending
transactions it would hold, so not those the transaction replaces. For
every staked entity and every payer that is not the sender:

- a `BANNED` one is refused with `ErrEntityBanned` [REPUTATION-010];
- a `THROTTLED` one is refused with `ErrEntityThrottled` if it is already
  an entity of `throttled_entity_mempool_count` pending transactions
  [REPUTATION-020];
- an unstaked payer that is not throttled or banned is refused with
  `ErrPayerOpsAllowed` if it already pays for $\mathit{opsAllowed} =
  \mathit{same\_unstaked\_entity\_mempool\_count} + \lfloor \mathit{included}
  \cdot \min(\mathit{included}, \mathit{max\_txs\_allowed\_unstaked\_entity})
  / \mathit{seen} \rfloor$ pending transactions, the term after the plus
  being 0 when `seen` is 0 [REPUTATION-220];

and a `THROTTLED` unstaked sender is refused with `ErrSenderThrottled` if it
already has `throttled_entity_mempool_count` pending transactions
[REPUTATION-210]. When the pool admits a transaction that replaces none, it
adds 1 to `seen` of each of its entities. A replacement adds 1 to `seen` of
each of its entities that is no entity of the transactions it replaces,
and takes 1 from each entity of those that is none of its own, so that only
a payer or factory it changes moves [REPUTATION-040]. When `reset` removes a
transaction under FP-15's step 1, which the model reads as its inclusion,
it adds 1 to `included` of each of its entities. A counter never goes below
0. At `reset`, after FP-15's steps, the pool removes every pending
transaction with an entity that is `BANNED` and is staked or a payer that is
not the sender [REPUTATION-010]. *Interpretation*, decision record 0061:
the `inclusionRate * min(...)` product is taken in integers as written; an
entity's state is checked against its counters as they stand when the
transaction arrives; and a limit counts what the pool would hold, as
LIFECYCLE-030's does.

**AM-14. Revalidation and blame.** At `reset` the pool revalidates as FP-15
does [LIFECYCLE-040], with FP-10's dependencies holding every slot any
validation frame read, of whatever account, and the code, balance and stake
of every entity, since under this ruleset a frame may read more than the
sender's storage. FP-15's step 3 re-runs what depends on state: AM-4's
step 6, the entities and their stake (AM-3), the budgets (AM-5) and the
simulation and trace (AM-7 to AM-9); its step 4 checks AM-12's exposure.
When a re-run fails, the entity it fails at is **blamed**: the entity of
the first validation frame that no longer passes, or the entity whose budget
is exceeded, or none for the `expiry_verify` frame. The `seen` of each other
entity of the transaction is reduced by 1, except a staked factory when the
sender is blamed [REPUTATION-320] and a staked sender [REPUTATION-330]; a
payer never loses reputation for another entity's failure [REPUTATION-310].
After the removals of FP-15 and AM-13, the pool removes, in the order
received and until none is left, every pending transaction of a sender that
lost one that no longer meets LIFECYCLE-010. Removals other than by a re-run
change no `seen`. *Interpretation*, decision record 0061: the ERC says what
a payer is spared and what a staked factory and sender absorb, so the reading
is that every entity but the blamed one is spared, unless it is a staked
factory or sender; and since "forming a nonce gap on any key is not
allowed" [LIFECYCLE-010], a removal at `reset` evicts what it leaves with a
gap, as a replacement does [LIFECYCLE-020].

**AM-15. Propagation.** [PROPAGATION-010] to [PROPAGATION-050] say how
transactions travel between nodes: with the block hash they were validated
against, validated before they are relayed, and with a sender dropped as a
spammer after a failure that holds at the hash it named. They are modules of
the descriptor and are not checked by the pool. A node applies them where it
relays, which `11-surface.md` specifies for the canonical mempool and not
for this one. *Not modelled.*

## What a sender is asked for

**AM-16. A fresh sender.** A sender with no stake, no reputation and no
transaction before is asked for nothing that the frame pool does not ask of
it already: its frames are bound by the 100000 gas budget of an unstaked
entity (AM-5), its storage by AM-9, its pending transactions by AM-10, and
a new entity is `ACTIVE` (AM-13). Stake and reputation bind it only once it
is a payer for others, a factory, or a staked sender. **Source:** the
ERC's Reputation section ("A new entity starts as `ACTIVE`") and its
general rules, which apply "to all staked entities and to unstaked
sponsoring payers". Derived, not chosen.

*Not modelled:* the hourly refresh as wall-clock time (the pool's own time
stands for it); `THROTTLED_ENTITY_BLOCK_COUNT` and
`THROTTLED_ENTITY_LIVE_BLOCKS`, which bind a builder and a pending
transaction's residency; and propagation (AM-15).
