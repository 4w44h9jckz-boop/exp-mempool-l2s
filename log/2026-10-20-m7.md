## Learned

- **M7 against "would show it wrong".** The commitment, the deadline and
  the tally are checked without reading the content:
  - LU-14 reads the ticket;
  - LU-15's steps 1 to 5 read the key message and the block;
  - LU-17's deadline is a time;
  - LU-18 reads votes and times.

  The vote bit itself is defined over a valid key message, and validity
  includes decryption. So a committee member does decrypt, but only a
  ciphertext whose key has already been released, which anyone holding
  the key message can decrypt too. The content is read after the fact is
  fixed, never before. The claim holds as the EIP is written.
- **A zero bit does not name who failed.** The votes cannot tell a key
  publisher that withheld from a sender whose ciphertext does not decrypt
  under the committed key. The released key message can: it is public,
  and it passes the key check. So "a withheld key shows up as a missing
  vote" holds, and naming the publisher needs the vote and the absence of
  a key message together (LU-19).
- The EIP's two tally conditions always leave a builder at least one
  treatment, and sometimes both. Which one a builder takes when both are
  allowed is not specified; the reference takes `observed`.

## Open questions

- The EIP gives the key observation deadline, `lucid_key_vote_deadline`
  and the attestation time names but no values. The vectors use times of
  their own.
- Bundles' key messages (a multi-key `commitment_key`) are left out, as in
  version 2.
- No chain runs EIP-8184. The key-publisher role is a rule applied to an
  observed block (skandha's `release`), not a service run against a
