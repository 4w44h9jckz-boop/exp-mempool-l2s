## Learned

- **EIP-2537's price settles the budget.** A pairing check of two pairs
  costs 102,900 gas, so no BLS check fits a 100,000-gas verify budget,
  whatever the rest of the frame costs.
- **A non-canonical paymaster's cost falls on results, not updates.** A
  committee's sequential updates lose nothing to FP-11's cap that FP-7 does
  not take already. Its independent results do.
- **Every transaction the simulator makes carries one nonce key.** So
  FP-3's key-set rules could not move any experiment.
