## Learned

- **The relaxations as first asked do not keep the bound.** Read against
  FP-10, FP-11 and FP-15, several pending per sender and the payer's count
  lifted each let one write invalidate many: a delegation, a sender slot,
  or a payment the pool does not hold. Decision record 0082 bounds them
  with a declared contract and a reserve, and experiment 27 measures both.
- **A violation shows in the access list, not the state.** A second write
  to an unset slot within a block leaves the state looking like a first
  write; only EIP-7928's per-transaction changes show two.
- **Two vector cases passed with a rule broken,** shown by porting them to
  skandha. They were tightened before publishing, in `833b5ec`.

## 13:15: M12 amended before merge

The coordinator's checks and requests, received after the pull requests
went up, change three things, in `c8470ef` and skandha `c38a849`:
- **RM-5's reserve is the larger of `max_cost` and $X$.** It replaces the
  refusal of a `max_cost` below $X$: a contract whose withdrawals pay out
  more than their gas has $X$ above `max_cost`, and every one of them was
  refused. The reserve counts in FP-11's check and again in FP-15 step 4.
  `ErrRelaxedReserve` is gone. Vectors first: four rewritten cases failed
  against the old reference. Two breaks, reserving `max_cost` and
  recounting with it, are each caught, and one case was tightened until the
  second was.
- **RM-8's relay carries claims only under a ticket scheme whose check is
  not a proof.** CG-10's pairing is one. A ticket that is itself a proof
  leaves a relay protocol-defined prefixes only.
- **RM-9's bound is on removals.** Experiment 27 now counts each write's
  re-runs: one payment out of the ticket paymaster removes one transaction
  and re-runs 65 prefixes, and none under a child descriptor that sets
  FP-15's `reuse_balance`. `relaxed.json` keeps it false, the canonical
  value.

skandha plays the vectors at `c8470ef`: 965 pass, 0 fail.
